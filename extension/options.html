How it works

Play a song or video on YouTube Music or YouTube and click the toolbar icon (or press Alt+Shift+D). It is converted by a small helper program running on your own computer and saved into the folder below. Everything here applies to every download — there is nothing to fill in per song. Choose whether a click gives you an MP3 or an MP4 under What to download.

Helper server

localhost and 127.0.0.1 always work. To run the server on another machine (e.g. a Raspberry Pi), enter its hostname or IP here — comma or space separated — then set the Server URL above to match.

Keep the helper server on a trusted network only: requests are plain HTTP, so anyone who can reach the host and knows the token can use it.

What to download

Every click downloads this kind of file. Switch to Video to save the picture as well; the audio and video settings below are kept separately, so you can flip between them without losing either.

Audio settings

A folder inside your browser's Downloads directory. Leave empty to save straight to Downloads. Subfolders are allowed (Music/YouTube); it is created automatically.

Use {title}, {artist} and {album}. For example {artist} - {title} or {title}.

Video settings

Videos get their own folder, so they do not mix with your music. Leave empty to save straight to Downloads.
